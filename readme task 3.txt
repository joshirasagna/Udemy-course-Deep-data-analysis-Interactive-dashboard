Udemy Courses Deep-Dive Analysis Dashboard
Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive analysis of Udemy course data using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to identify trends in course popularity, pricing, subscriber engagement, and revenue opportunities through KPI tracking, pivot table analysis, and an interactive dashboard.
Objectives
* Analyze Udemy course performance. 
* Measure audience engagement and course popularity. 
* Evaluate pricing strategies across courses. 
* Create business insights through data visualization. 
* Build an interactive dashboard for decision-making. 
Tools Used
* Microsoft Excel 
* Pivot Tables 
* Pivot Charts 
* Slicers 
* KPI Cards 
* Dashboard Design 
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
KPIFormulaBusiness MeaningTotal CoursesCount of CoursesPlatform sizeTotal SubscribersSum of SubscribersAudience reachAverage PriceAverage PricePricing strategyAverage Reviews per CourseReviews ÷ CoursesEngagement levelPaid Course PercentagePaid Courses ÷ Total Courses × 100Revenue focus
Analysis Performed
1. Popularity Analysis
Courses were categorized into:
* High Popularity 
* Medium Popularity 
* Low Popularity 
Distribution:
* High Popularity: 257 Courses 
* Medium Popularity: 1,515 Courses 
* Low Popularity: 1,906 Courses 
2. Pricing Analysis
Average course price was analyzed to understand pricing patterns and their relationship with course popularity.
3. Review & Engagement Analysis
Course reviews were evaluated to measure learner engagement and satisfaction.
4. Dashboard Development
An interactive dashboard was created featuring:
* KPI Cards 
* Popularity Distribution Charts 
* Pricing Insights 
* Dynamic Filters/Slicers 
* User-Friendly Visualizations 
Key Insights
* Most courses fall into the Low and Medium popularity categories. 
* Highly popular courses generally command higher average prices. 
* Reviews provide a strong indicator of course engagement and success. 
* Dashboard visualizations enable quick performance monitoring and decision-making.
